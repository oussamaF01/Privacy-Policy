# Privacy Policy

**Simply Yummy**
**Contact:** oussama.farhani@ept.ucartn
**Last updated:** November 21, 2026

## 1. About us

Simply Yummy is a food recipe page. We share easy, tasty recipe ideas and use the Pinterest API to publish our recipe video pins to Simply Yummy's own Pinterest account and boards. The Simply Yummy publishing tool is used only by our team, for our own account. It is not a public service, and it is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Pinterest.

## 2. What data our app accesses

Our app connects to the Simply Yummy Pinterest account through the official Pinterest API and OAuth. We use this access only to:

- read the list of our boards;
- create boards in our account, when needed;
- upload videos and create pins (title, description, link, alt text and video) in our account.

## 3. What data we collect

We do **not** collect, store, or process personal data about our visitors, followers, or any other person through this app. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

The only data the app handles is:

- our own Pinterest access token, which is stored securely on our own computer and is never published or shared;
- the pin content we choose to publish (titles, descriptions, links, and videos).

## 4. How data is used and shared

Data is used only to create pins on our own Pinterest account. We do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or machine learning. Data sent to Pinterest is handled under [Pinterest's Privacy Policy](https://policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy).

## 5. Third-party content and credit

Some of our pins feature recipe videos made by other creators. We credit the original creator (for example, their TikTok username) in the pin description and link back to the original video. If you are a creator and would like a pin credited differently or removed, contact us (see section 8) and we will handle your request promptly.

## 6. Data retention and security

The app keeps no database of personal data. The access token and a local progress file (a list of pins already posted) stay on our computer. We protect the access token like a password and revoke it if it may have been exposed.

## 7. Revoking access

Simply Yummy can revoke the app's access to our Pinterest account at any time in Pinterest under *Settings > Apps*, or by deleting the app in the Pinterest developer portal. Deleting the local token file also stops the app from accessing the account.

## 8. Your rights and requests

If you have a question about this policy, or you are a content creator who wants a pin removed or changed, email us at oussama.farhani@ept.ucartn.

## 9. Children

Our app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If our app changes in a way that affects how data is handled, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date above.
